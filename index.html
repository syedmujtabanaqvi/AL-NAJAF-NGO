Our story

Built one camp at a time, since 2008.

Al Najaf Foundation started in 2008 as a handful of volunteers running weekend health checkups in one neighbourhood. Today we operate year-round: medical camps, emergency relief drives, and trained security teams that support public events and crisis response.

Our work stands on one belief a community is strongest when it looks after its own. Every camp, every relief drop, every shift on duty is logged, reported, and open to the people we serve.
