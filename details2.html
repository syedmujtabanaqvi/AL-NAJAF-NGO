About The Medical Assistance Program

Al Najaf Foundation ke under chalne wala Medical Assistance Program un Tamam mustahiq afrad ko target karta hai jo high medical treatment costs ki wajah se apna checkup nahi karwa pate. Hum professional doctors, qualified staff, aur lab technicians ke sath mil kar weekend aur weekly bases par free medical camps set up karte hain.

Har camp me general health checkup ke sath sath specialized care jese Eye Checkups, Blood Pressure, Diabetes Testing, aur Free Medicine Distribution shamil hoti hai.

Key Features & Services Included

  • Free General Physician Consultation
  • Free Prescription Medicines
  • Eye Specialist Camps & Spectacles
  • Diabetes & Hypertension Screening
  • Mobile Medical Unit for Remote Areas
  • Emergency First Aid & Patient Referral

How Our Medical Camp Operates

01

Area Survey & Need Assessment

Team pehlay zarooratmand ilaqon ka survey karti hai jahan healthcare facilities kam hon.

02

Medical Team & Medicine Setup

Qualified doctors, medics, aur required medicines ka stock mobilize kiya jata hai.

03

Patient Registration & Triage

Ground volunteers patients ko register karte hain aur orderly fashion me doctors tak bhejte hain.

04

Free Diagnosis & Pharmacy

Doctor ke checkup ke baad exact prescribed medicines free pharmacy counter se di jati hain.
