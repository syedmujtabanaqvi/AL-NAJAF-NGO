16 Years of Unbroken Service

Our Complete History (2008 – 2026)

Amaan Foundation ki bunyaad se lekar aaj tak ki mukammal tareekh: kaise ek chota sa medical camp aage chalkar hazaron logon ke liye relief aur security ka zariya bana.

MARCH 2008

Pehla Free Medical Camp

Karachi ke ek chote se ilaqay me 4 zimmadar volunteers ne mil kar pehla free health checkup camp lagaya. Basic medicines aur doctor consultation ke sath 120 patients ko treat kiya gaya.

Volunteers: 4
Patients Served: 120+
OCTOBER 2011

Disaster Relief Wings ki Bunyaad

Moosamiyati tabahiyan aur emergency situations ko dekhte hue foundation ne relief team ki shuruaat ki. Paani, khurak, aur emergency medical kits faraham karne ka silsila regularize hua.

Ration Packs Distributed: 1,500+
JANUARY 2013

Community Security Division

Public events, majalis, aur outdoor gatherings me crowd management aur security ke liye trained volunteers ki team formally organize ki gayi.

Security Operations: 15+
NOVEMBER 2016

Free Eye & Specialized Camps

General medical checkups ke ilawa Eye Specialist aur Blood Testing camps ki shuruaat ki gayi, jahan muft spectacles aur eye care faraham ki gayi.

Total Camps Reached: 50+
JUNE 2019

100th Medical Camp Milestone

Foundation ne apna 100th medical camp pura kiya aur sath hi pehli Mobile Health Unit van ka aaghaz kiya taake door-daraaz ilaqon tak medical service pahunchai ja sake.

Patients Reached: 50,000+
AUGUST 2022

Sindh Flood Relief Drive

Thatta, Badin, aur Hyderabad ke aas pass ke ilaqon me flood-affected families ke liye medical support aur winter relief camps qaim kiye gaye.

Families Assisted: 10,000+
FEBRUARY 2026

500+ Medical Camps Completed

Aaj Amaan Foundation 12 alag zilaon (districts) me continuous medical camps, annual winter drives, aur complete security coverage deliver kar raha hai.

Total Beneficiaries: 120,000+
Districts Covered: 12
